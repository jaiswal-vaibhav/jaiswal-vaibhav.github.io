About

I am a senior researcher at the French Authority for Nuclear Safety and Radiation Protection (ASNR), working in reactor physics, neutronics, and criticality safety. My work focuses on how nuclear data and their uncertainties influence neutron transport calculations and the assessment of nuclear systems.

With more than ten years of research experience, I bring together experimental measurements, nuclear data evaluation, and computational modelling. A particular focus is thermal neutron scattering, alongside sensitivity analysis and uncertainty quantification. My aim is to strengthen the physical basis and reliability of calculations used in reactor studies and criticality safety assessments.

I contribute to the JEFF nuclear data project and international research collaborations, and supervise doctoral researchers, postdoctoral fellows, and interns. My research career spans CEA, IRSN, and ASNR, following a PhD at the University of Lille.

Ongoing · International collaboration

NCSP: Nuclear Data Processing Intercomparison

I am leading nuclear-data intercomparison activities within the Nuclear Criticality Safety Program (NCSP) to assess how nuclear cross-section processing parameters affect criticality safety calculations.

The work brings together international partners, including Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL), Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL), and Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL).

Related presentation: Impact of Nuclear Data Processing Parameters on Cross Sections (HAL)

In progress · Research methods

Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Nuclear Data

Together with my postdoctoral researcher, I am developing physics-informed neural network approaches to improve nuclear cross-section data, with a particular focus on thermal scattering cross sections. Our ongoing work explores how physical constraints can guide data modelling and refinement.

Ongoing · APRENDE Task 4.2

Developing TARA for Thermal Scattering Uncertainty Analysis

Building on funding secured in 2023 for nuclear-data uncertainty research, I am supervising Aitor Bengoechea Fernández’s postdoctoral work at ASNR on the development of TARA within the European APRENDE project.

TARA is a Python-based tool intended for open-source release. It generates physically constrained random thermal scattering law files to study how uncertainties propagate to neutron cross sections and reactor physics and criticality safety calculations.

Current Research Supervision

  • 2025–present Aitor Bengoechea Fernández Postdoctoral supervision

    Postdoctoral researcher at ASNR, working on thermal scattering law uncertainties through the TARA project.

    Project background: In 2023, I secured funding for research on nuclear-data uncertainties and the improvement of thermal scattering cross sections. This work led to the TARA project, which is now being developed with Aitor Bengoechea Fernández within APRENDE Task 4.2.

    Research: TARA — Thermal scattering law uncertainty Analysis using RAndom files. Together, we are developing a Python tool intended for open-source release to generate physically constrained random thermal scattering law files and assess their impact on neutron cross sections, reactor physics, and criticality safety calculations.

    The work forms part of APRENDE Task 4.2 on thermal neutron scattering. It examines how sampling choices affect uncertainty estimates, using experimental and simulation data to guide physically meaningful variations in moderator materials.

    Research output: Co-author of Procedural Choices Determine Thermal Scattering Law Uncertainties, an SSRN preprint using graphite as a case study.

Research Topics

⚛️

Nuclear Data Development & Processing

Nuclear data processing, evaluation, and validation using codes such as NJOY, GAIA, and FUDGE for reactor physics and criticality safety applications.

🌡️

Thermal Scattering Law (TSL)

Development and assessment of thermal scattering laws based on time-of-flight experiments and molecular dynamics simulations to improve neutron transport calculations at thermal energies.

📊

Uncertainty Quantification & Covariance

Uncertainty quantification, covariance development, and uncertainty propagation for nuclear data in reactor physics and criticality safety applications.

🔬

Verification & Validation

Cross-section verification and validation studies using ICSBEP and IRPhE benchmarks, supporting neutronics and criticality safety analyses.

🌐

International Nuclear Data Projects

Active contribution to the JEFF nuclear data library and international expert groups, supporting the development and maintenance of nuclear data evaluations.

Master’s Internship

2027 · 6 months

Thermal Scattering and Criticality Safety: Exploring the Role of Phonon Spectra

Location: ASNR, Fontenay-aux-Roses, France
Team: Neutronics Laboratory (LN), Neutronics and Criticality Safety Department (SNC)
Expected start: Around February 2027

Assess the impact of new thermal scattering law evaluations on criticality safety calculations, and investigate how features of moderator phonon spectra affect the effective multiplication factor, keff.

The work combines ICSBEP benchmark simulations with MORET 6 and numerical studies with OpenMC, varying phonon-band energies and relative weights. The internship offers experience in nuclear data processing with NJOY and scientific programming with Python.

Candidate profile: Master 2 or equivalent engineering-school level, preferably in nuclear engineering. Knowledge of Python or C++ is appreciated.
